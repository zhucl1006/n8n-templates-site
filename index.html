Public procurementAlerts

UK Tender Alerts

Get notified about new UK public-sector contract awards that match your keywords. On a weekday schedule (or on demand) the workflow pulls recent notices from UK Contracts Finder via an Apify Actor, removes notices you have already seen, optionally filters by minimum value, and posts a concise alert to your Slack channel (Feishu / DingTalk supported with a small payload change).

Weekday schedule→ Your keywords→ Apify: UK Contracts Finder→ Dedupe & filter→ Slack alert
  • Configurable keywords, look-back window and optional minimum award value
  • Alerts list title, buyer, supplier, award value and a link to each notice
  • Remembers notices already sent and only posts when there is something new

You provide: Apify token, Slack/Feishu/DingTalk webhook URL, n8n (Cloud or self-hosted).

Domain Lead Enrichment

Turn a plain list of company domains into a structured lead sheet. The workflow looks up WHOIS / RDAP registration data for each domain and can optionally extract publicly listed contact details from the company's own website, then merges everything into one clean JSON file — or straight into Google Sheets.

Domain list→ Apify: WHOIS / RDAP→ Optional: public contact page→ Merge→ JSON / Sheets
  • Registrar, creation & expiry dates, days until expiry, name servers, registrant organisation & country
  • Two modes: WHOIS only, or WHOIS + public contact extraction
  • Contact mode adds publicly listed emails, phone numbers and social profiles

You provide: Apify token, your own domain list, n8n (Cloud or self-hosted). For lawful lead research on domains you are allowed to process — not for unsolicited bulk email.

FAQ

Do I need an Apify account?

Yes. The templates call public Apify Actors using your own Apify API token, so data runs and usage fees stay on your account. Apify offers a free tier that is enough for testing.

Does it work with n8n Cloud and self-hosted n8n?

Yes. Any recent n8n version with the standard HTTP Request node and Header Auth credentials will work.

How do I receive the files?

Right after payment you get a download link (and an email receipt) from our checkout provider, Creem. The download is a ZIP containing the workflow file, setup guide and example inputs.

Can I modify the workflows?

Absolutely. You can customise them freely for use by yourself or within your team / organisation. Reselling or redistributing the templates is not permitted — see the license.

What is your refund policy?

If a template cannot be imported or does not work as described and we cannot fix it, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. Details are in our Refund Policy.

Do you store my API keys or data?

No. Credentials live in your own n8n instance and the workflows run on your infrastructure. We never see your Apify token, webhook URLs or results.

Can the enrichment template be used for cold emailing?

The template is meant for lawful research on domains you are permitted to process. You must comply with anti-spam and privacy laws in your jurisdiction; using the output for unsolicited bulk email is prohibited by our terms.
